DrivingCoach Privacy Policy

DrivingCoach is designed to be private by default. Your driving data stays on your device.

Private by default

Last updated: 16 June 2026

DrivingCoach is a driving-practice and self-assessment aid. It helps learner drivers practise routes, observe hazards, and review driving behaviour while keeping personal data on the device.

Data stored on your device only

All driving data is stored locally and is not uploaded to our server.

  • Location GPS: used for routes, speed, distance, and location-based warnings.
  • Camera: road-facing analysis is processed on-device for signs and hazards.
  • Microphone: used only if voice commands are enabled.
  • Drive history: routes, scores, observations, hazards, and snapshots remain local.

We do not sell, share, or transmit your personal data, images, location, or audio. There are no analytics or advertising trackers.

You control your data

  • Export: export your data from Settings → Data & Sync.
  • Delete: delete drives, routes, images, or uninstall the app to remove local data.

Third-party services

When online, the app may request limited data from:

  • OpenStreetMap / CARTO for map tiles
  • OSRM for matching routes to roads
  • OpenStreetMap Overpass API for speed-limit lookup
  • TensorFlow.js / MediaPipe model CDNs for downloading AI models

No account identity is attached to these requests.

Safety notice

DrivingCoach is advisory only. It is not a substitute for attentive driving, a qualified instructor, or the official driving exam. Always follow real traffic signs, road rules, and examiner instructions.

Disclaimer & limitation of liability

DrivingCoach is provided free of charge, “as is”, without warranty. It does not guarantee exam success or driving competence and is not affiliated with any driving school, examiner, or government authority.

Do not interact with the app while driving. You are solely responsible for safe and lawful driving.

Children

The app is intended for learner drivers and adults. It is not directed at children under 13.
