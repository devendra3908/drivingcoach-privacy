Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 June 2026

DrivingCoach is a driving-practice and self-assessment aid. It is designed to be private by default: your driving data stays on your device.

Data stored on your device only

All of your data is stored locally on your device and is not uploaded to us or any server:

  • Location (GPS): tracks your drive, matches your route, measures speed/distance, and triggers location-based warnings.
  • Camera: the road-facing camera is analysed on-device to detect signs, pedestrians, cyclists and hazards. Snapshots are saved only on your device.
  • Microphone: used only if you enable voice commands, processed by your device's speech recognition.
  • Drive history, scores, routes, observations, hazards: stored locally.

We do not sell, share, or transmit your personal data, images, location, or audio to our servers. There are no analytics or advertising trackers.

You control your data

  • Export: export your data and captured images from Settings → Data & Sync.
  • Delete: deleting a drive/route, clearing images, or uninstalling the app removes the associated data from your device.

Third-party services (network requests)

When online, the app may request data from these services to function. Only the information necessary for the request is sent; no account or identity is attached:

  • OpenStreetMap / CARTO — map tiles.
  • OSRM — snapping imported routes to roads.
  • OpenStreetMap Overpass API — looking up the road speed limit near you.
  • TensorFlow.js / MediaPipe model CDNs — downloading the AI models once.

These are governed by their own privacy policies.

Safety notice

The app is advisory only — a practice aid, not a substitute for attentive driving, a qualified instructor, or the official driving exam. Always follow real traffic signs, road rules, and your examiner's instructions. Camera AI may be inaccurate.

Children

The app is intended for learner drivers and adults; it is not directed at children under 13.
